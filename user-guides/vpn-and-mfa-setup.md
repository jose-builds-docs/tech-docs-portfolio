# Employee Guide: Setting Up Corporate VPN & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Welcome! This guide will walk you through setting up your secure remote connection and protecting your company account using Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). 

---

## Part 1: What is MFA and Why Do We Use It?
Multi-Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. Instead of just a password, you will confirm your identity using a trusted device (like your smartphone) whenever you log in. This protects company data from unauthorized access.

---

## Part 2: Setting Up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

### Step 2.1: Register Your Authenticator App
1. Open a web browser on your computer and navigate to your corporate account portal (e.g., `myaccount.microsoft.com`).
2. Log in using your temporary corporate email and initial password provided by IT.
3. Click on **Security Info** on the left menu, then click **Add sign-in method**.
4. Choose **Authenticator app** from the dropdown menu and click **Add**.

### Step 2.2: Pair Your Smartphone
1. On your smartphone, download and install **Microsoft Authenticator** (available for iOS and Android in your app store).
2. Open the app, tap the **`+` (plus)** icon to add an account, and select **Work or school account**.
3. Scan the **QR Code** displayed on your computer screen using your phone's camera.
4. Once scanned, click **Next** on your computer. A test notification will be sent to your phone—tap **Approve** to complete the setup.

---

## Part 3: Installing and Connecting to the Corporate VPN

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) encrypts your internet traffic when working remotely, allowing you to access internal company resources securely.

### Step 3.1: Install the VPN Client
1. Visit the company software portal or IT self-service center to download the **Enterprise VPN Client** for your operating system (Windows or macOS).
2. Run the installer package and follow the standard on-screen prompts (**Next > Next > Finish**).

### Step 3.2: Connect to the Network
1. Launch the VPN application from your desktop or taskbar.
2. Enter the corporate gateway address provided by IT (e.g., `vpn.enterprise.com`).
3. Log in using your corporate username and password.
4. When prompted, complete your **MFA approval** on your mobile authenticator app.
5. Verify that the app status changes to **Connected** before opening internal tools.
